Privacy Policy – Leonida Madness Uploader

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Who uses this app

Leonida Madness Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Leonida Madness YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

The app uses Google OAuth to access the owner's own YouTube account, with permission to upload videos and set their titles, descriptions and tags. It does not access any other Google data and does not access data belonging to anyone else.

How data is used and stored

The OAuth sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer and is used only to upload videos to the owner's channel. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time from Google account permissions.
